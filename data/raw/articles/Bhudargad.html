Bhudargad is a tehsil in Kolhapur district in the Indian state of Maharashtra.[1] Bhudargad is a fort built by Shivaji.[citation needed] Gargoti, the administrative place of Bhudargad tehsil is also known as an educational and economic hub of tehsil that has various institutions such as Mouni Vidyapeeth, D.Ed and B.Ed. College and ICRE - Institute of Civil and Rural Engineering.
